# Privacy

Derelict has no accounts, no analytics and no server of its own.

- **Your progress** (XP, confirmed and removed places) is stored only on your device.
- **Your location** is used on your device to find nearby places, plan routes and check you in.
- **Third-party services** receive coordinates when you use the app: Overpass (place search), Nominatim (address lookup), the FOSSGIS/OSRM routing servers (directions) and OpenStreetMap tile servers (map images). Each has its own privacy policy. Your IP address is visible to them, as with any website.
- **Opening your maps app** hands the destination to whichever app you choose.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).
